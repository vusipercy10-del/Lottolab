TRIPLETS → GAPS → REPEATS → TRANSITIONS → POOLS → MASTER SCORE →

## Master score
The default weighting in the project is a ranking aid, not a probability model:
- Frequency: 25%
- Multiple overlap: 15%
- Mathematical type: 10%
- Category overlap: 10%
- Zone balance: 10%
- Last digit: 10%
- Pair strength: 8%
- Triplet strength: 5%
- Transition/repeat: 7%

## Important interpretation
These analyses describe historical structure. They do not establish that a future lottery draw is predictable or that any number has a guaranteed advantage.

## Recommended workflow
1. Select a game.
2. Import historical results.
3. Validate the draw width and number range.
4. Choose a history window: 10, 12, 20, 50 or 100.
5. Compare recent and long-term results.
6. Review number, mathematical, multiple and ending analyses.
7. Review transition and overlap results.
8. Generate pools.
9. Generate candidate combinations.
10. Run walk-forward backtesting.
11. Compare the strategy against simpler baselines before relying on it.
